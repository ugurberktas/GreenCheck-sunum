Our Solution

"We turn banks' existing field staff into digital ESG verifiers — no new hires, no new departments."

GreenCheck embeds into existing bank operations. When an SME applies for credit, the nearest field officer completes a structured 5-10 minute ESG checklist during their regular visit. GPS-verified. Photo-evidenced. Instantly scored.
